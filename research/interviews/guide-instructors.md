# Interview guide: instructors and program leads (VP-003)

Goal: learn how instructors run, check and grade customer discovery today, what breaks, and who decides on tools and budget. Tests VP3 and VP6, plus VP1 from the reviewer's side ([value-prop.md](../value-prop.md)).

**Do not pitch First Ten.** Ask about last term's real course, not about a tool you're building. If they ask, describe it briefly at the end.

## Who to recruit (3-5)

- Faculty who teach entrepreneurship, product, or AI courses where students must talk to customers (for example, Babson faculty and AI Academy colleagues).
- At least 1 program or center lead (accelerator, venture lab, I-Corps-style program) who influences tool choices or budget.
- If possible, 1 person from outside your own school.

## Before you start

- 20-30 minutes. Notes in First Ten's interview mode or a doc.
- Say: "Is it okay if I take notes? I'll refer to you by role, not name, unless you tell me otherwise." Record what they agree to.

## Questions (past behavior only)

| # | Question | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Tell me about the last course or cohort where students had to talk to customers. How was it set up? | Context |
| I2 | Walk me through how one team's customer discovery went from the assignment to a grade last term. | VP3 |
| I3 | How did you know the interviews actually happened, and with real potential customers? Was there a time you weren't sure? | VP6 |
| I4 | Has AI changed what students hand in for discovery? Tell me about a recent example. | VP6, A5 |
| I5 | When did you last realize a team was stuck? How did you find out, and how late was it? | VP3, VP4 |
| I6 | What do you use today to collect or review discovery work: the LMS, spreadsheets, LaunchPad Central, docs? What's annoying about it? | Alternatives |
| I7 | What does good evidence look like when you grade it? Could you share a rubric or an example with names removed? | VP1, Evidence Pack |
| I8 | Roughly how much time a week goes into reviewing discovery work during the busiest part of the term? | VP3 intensity |
| I9 | Have you tried any tool for this in the last two years? How was it chosen, who paid, and how did it go? | Buyer, budget |
| I10 | Who else has a say in which tools a course or program uses here? | Buyer map |
| I11 | Who else should I talk to about this? | Referral |

**Closing ask (a commitment)**: "Would you be willing to share a de-identified rubric or assignment so I can see how you assess discovery?" Note the answer: yes now, yes later, or no.

## What to listen for

- **Specific incidents** (a team that faked or skipped interviews, a late surprise) beat general complaints.
- **Time and money already spent** on the problem: tools bought, TAs assigned, rubric rewrites. That's real pain; "it would be nice" isn't.
- **Who actually decides and pays**: instructor, department, center, IT procurement.
- **Privacy constraints**: what instructors can see about students' interviewees.
