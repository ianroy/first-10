# Interview guide: instructors and program leads

**VP-008 · protocol 1.0 · 2026-10-18.** Human-run VP-003 guide; no interviews completed here. Follow the [value-test protocol](../value-test-plan.md) and [shared note template](README.md#private-note-template). Target 3–5 people with a real customer-discovery assignment/review in the past 12 months. Seek a person who can describe a past approval process; reviewer, champion and buyer may be different people.

## Before the interview

Record role, broad course/program context, recruiting source, researcher relationship and prior product exposure. Do not assume a workshop attendee represents an institution, has a review problem or controls budget. Record outside-institution and purchasing-process coverage or their absence.

Plan 20–30 minutes, with flexible pacing/format. Use private manual notes by default. Explain the actual named access, storage, retention and withdrawal arrangements. Obtain separate choices for manual notes, optional external AI, each selected sharing audience and follow-up. The app checkbox is not this record. Do not record audio/video or request identifiable student work, grades, customer details or institutional credentials. A redacted rubric/assignment is optional; the instructor must be able to authorize the specific use.

Opening: “I’m learning how entrepreneurship educators set and review customer-discovery work today. Please walk me through a recent example, including anything that already works well. This is voluntary; you can skip questions or stop.” Handle note permission before taking content notes. Do not show First Ten or name a preferred diagnosis before their account.

Do not start with “How do you prove interviews happened?” or “What’s annoying about your LMS?” A teacher’s need for independent authenticity evidence would expose a product limitation; a timestamp or quote does not establish authenticity. A useful current rubric/workflow is a valid counterexample to the proposed product.

## Optional artifact review and close

Ask “What else should I understand about that example?” If the participant offers an artifact, ask whether they can show a redacted version for the specified private review. Preview any excerpt and its audience before retaining/sharing it. Log `offered`, `agreed`, `received`, `declined` or `unknown` with dates; offered-but-undelivered stays pending. Receipt of a rubric is a research action, not purchase evidence.

Only after this neutral discussion may a separately labeled concept/reviewer-message study occur. Use a real cleared record for VP-009; a fictional StudyLoop rehearsal never counts as teacher-review evidence. Ask what feedback decision they make with the material and what remains missing; do not ask for a testimonial or infer classroom adoption from interest.

Record an explicit follow-up choice privately. Do not contact referrals automatically or retain third-party contact details in research notes. At coding, keep reviewer value, champion interest, known authority, reported approval process, actual spend and completed adoption steps as separate fields. Code irrelevant founder hypotheses `no_signal`; do not fill them from general faculty opinions about students.
