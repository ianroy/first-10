No interviews, outcomes, competitor hands-on tests or purchasing evidence were produced by this desk audit.

Use these classifications:

- **Current:** present in the inspected source, with the stated limits; verify the deployed revision before demonstrating it.
- **External context:** supported by the named primary source or explicitly attributed owner report; not First Ten outcome evidence.
- **Candidate:** a value or market hypothesis to test, not a measured result.
- **Planned:** an identified task with no implemented feature implied.
- **Retired:** removed from reusable positioning because unsupported, overstated or contradicted by the narrow source check.

## 5. Copy handoff and change control

The following can guide WEB-001/004 and EDU-001/002; it is not an efficacy claim or proof of a deployed page change:

- **Factual product description:** “A guided customer-discovery workspace for student founders, with editable questions, notes, AI-assisted summaries and experiments.” (C01–C04.)
- **Founder aim:** “Turn real customer conversations into a clearer next move.” Follow with what the app does and a human-review boundary. (V01.)
- **Educator aim:** “Help students show how customer evidence changes their next decision.” Explain the proposed classroom exercise and distinguish planned teacher tools. (V02, V07.)
- **Demonstration label:** “Illustrative example” or “Sample data.” Do not attach fabricated testimonial names, institutional logos or outcome counts. (E03–E04.)
- **Current AI boundary:** “AI drafts. You review and decide.” Explain external processing where notes are collected or AI is requested. (C07–C09.)
- **Planned capability label:** “Planned: teacher review and class workflows.” Update only after implementation and verification; a roadmap entry is insufficient. (V07.)

Public-copy owners should map each consequential capability, outcome, comparative, privacy, price or endorsement claim to a ledger ID. Qualify candidate benefits, remove unsupported assertions, and avoid repeating long caveats in every sentence. One clear distinction between today's product, the teaching concept and planned tools is better than blending their statuses.

To change a claim's status, append a dated source/revision, what was observed, relevant limits and the owner/test that supports the change. A workshop reaction is a reaction; a chosen next step is a next step; a completed classroom pilot is a pilot. None automatically establishes learning effectiveness, product-market fit or purchasing intent. VP-008, VP-003 and the founder pilot retain these open questions.
