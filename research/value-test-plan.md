# First Ten value-test protocol

**VP-008 · version 1.0 · 2026-10-18 · prospective internal protocol.** Baseline `b4e3110a0356cd26c0d80ac00916b2c293607ee9`. These rules are specified before participant collection; this is not an external preregistration or a completed study. No recruiting, interview, pilot, message test or outcome occurred in this task. [Claim ledger](claim-ledger.md) · [founder guide](interviews/guide-founders.md) · [instructor guide](interviews/guide-instructors.md) · [log and coding template](interviews/README.md) · [human recruiting materials](recruitment-materials.md).

## 1. Question, sample and sequence

Test whether a student founder with a working app and limited recurring use benefits from a conversation → explained decision → next action loop. Access, time, method, confidence, readiness and interpretation are competing explanations. Keep private decision value, reporting value, educator review, champion interest and purchasing authority separate. Current app capabilities are in [ProductSpec](../ProductSpec.md); proposed concepts remain concepts.

**Founder eligibility, operational choice for this first sample:** adult student (18+); a live app or working interactive prototype that a potential user can actually try; a specific recruitment effort in the last 30 days; and few or no recurring users. For consistent screening, “few” means a self-reported 0–10 distinct people who used it on at least two separate occasions in the previous 14 days. Record the basis and uncertainty; this cutoff is a sampling choice, not a market boundary or verified analytics. If recurring use cannot be estimated, mark eligibility unresolved until clarified, not zero. A recruitment effort may have stalled at access, but must include a concrete attempted step and date/context, not only a future intention. A landing page, idea-only project or hypothetical customer story is an adjacent case, not an eligible founder note.

Recruit **5–8 eligible founders**: first three in R0-004a, then another two to five in R0-004b. After three, record gaps and any prospective amendment before the next cohort. Aim for at least two course and two self-directed founders overall; include a useful warm-contact case and an effective existing-workflow counterexample when recruitable. Mixed contexts are labeled mixed and not counted as both quotas. Disclose gaps rather than inventing coverage. Friends can be relevant; record the relationship and recruitment source. Do not select only enthusiastic workshop attendees or people already asking for this product.

Recruit **3–5 instructors/program leads** with a real customer-discovery assignment or review episode in the last 12 months. Seek at least one person who can describe an actual tool-approval process; this does not make them the buyer. Reviewer, champion, budget authority and process observer are separately recorded roles. An outside-institution perspective is desirable, not fabricated if unavailable. Workshop participants are a convenience sample; attendance and faculty status do not establish adoption, authority or willingness to pay.

The human study owner conducts recruiting and interviews. The agent prepares materials and may organize only material cleared for that specific AI processing. No automated outreach or audio/video recording is included. For students taught or graded by the researcher, use an independent invitation/interviewer where possible; participation, declining and choosing the manual route must not affect grades. Record the relationship and any course requirement.

## 2. Participant choices and data handling

Before human collection, record the named study owner/interviewer, private storage location, named people with access, contact/withdrawal route and the retention dates. Give participants this information in plain language. Unfilled logistics are a human-study prerequisite, not permission to collect first and decide later. Use a restricted non-public store outside Git; disable automatic transcription/AI in the chosen note tool. Repository files contain templates and approved non-identifying methodological summaries, not raw participant notes, consent records, private artifacts or contact keys.

Each permission has its own `granted | declined | unknown | withdrawn` state, date, protocol version, scope and who recorded it:

| Choice                    | Plain-language request / scope                                                                                                                                                                                     | Default and rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Manual notes              | “May I take notes about your experience for our internal product research? [Named people] can read them in [private location]. You can skip anything or stop.” Explain the dated retention and withdrawal process. | Unknown until an explicit answer. Declining notes means no retained interview content, including reconstructed notes afterward. Participation logistics can be recorded without content.                                                   |
| External AI processing    | Optional, separate request for specified selected/redacted material, purpose, named service/provider route and known handling/retention limits                                                                     | Default no AI. Permission for OpenRouter/model processing does not authorize another assistant/service. Giving notes to a coding assistant is AI processing too. If the route/policy is unknown or permission is absent, analyze manually. |
| Quote or artifact sharing | Preview the exact selected excerpt/artifact, de-identification and audience: named reviewer/class, private repository, pitch or public website                                                                     | Each audience/use needs an explicit choice. Internal notes permission is not public-quote or instructor-sharing permission. No blanket request for all future uses.                                                                        |
| Follow-up                 | Optional contact for the specified day-seven/day-fourteen or artifact-review purpose                                                                                                                               | A scheduling agreement is logistics, not product demand. Contact details stay in the separate private scheduling record.                                                                                                                   |

The app’s note-taking checkbox is local UI state, not the research permission record and not permission for provider processing or sharing. Historical missing choices stay unknown. Permission from a founder does not automatically cover identifying customer/student material they possess. Ask for their own account or a safely redacted artifact; do not collect third-party names, contact details, grades or sensitive content. First names alone are not anonymization.

Retain raw/private notes only as long as needed for this round: review access and deletion **30 days after final follow-up**, and delete the working notes/artifacts by **90 days after final follow-up** unless a new, documented purpose and participant choice are established before extension. Delete the contact key within 30 days of final follow-up unless a separate follow-up is explicitly agreed. These are proposed study operating limits, not claims about provider retention or automated product deletion. Log actual review/deletion completion. Keep only non-identifying aggregate methods/decisions beyond that point.

On withdrawal, stop the relevant collection, AI processing and future sharing; remove the material from the working dataset and revise affected findings. Ask which permissions are withdrawn without requiring a reason. Explain before collection that previously viewed/downloaded copies or material already processed externally cannot be guaranteed recall; do not promise deletion from Git history or provider systems. Anonymous participation/withdrawal totals may remain as disclosed methods data, with no content or identity. Use fresh permission before any new purpose. No participant content should enter versioned repository history in this protocol.

## 3. Evidence identifiers and coding

Assign role-only ids `F001…` and `I001…` after screening; retain any identity mapping privately. A pilot reuses the same founder id. Examples of identifier shapes (not observations): `F001-E01` episode, `F001-E01-O01` observation, `F001-A01` permitted artifact, `F001-D07-O01` follow-up. Prefix fictional exercises `DEMO-`; they never enter research tallies. Every record states date, question id, exact quote versus paraphrase, self-report versus directly observed behavior, permission scope and assistance.

Separate three layers: **what was observed/reported**, **our interpretation**, **what remains unknown**. A supplied quote is exact only if written accurately with permission; otherwise label it a paraphrase. Do not turn an AI summary into participant testimony. Record a customer request as `requested`, `agreed`, `completed`, `declined`, `pending` or `unknown`, with source/date. A promised trial, offered introduction, follow-up agreement, compliment or hypothetical willingness is not completed use, payment or purchase evidence.

Code each hypothesis for each eligible participant using one of:

- `supports`: a specific relevant episode/action supports the narrow hypothesis; cite observation ids and alternatives.
- `contradicts`: a specific episode supplies a meaningful counterexample; cite its context, not just an absence of a positive statement.
- `no_signal`: not probed, not applicable or no concrete material; record which reason. Never convert silence into contradiction.
- `uncertain`: relevant material exists but is ambiguous, inconsistent or equally compatible with competing explanations. Preserve both sides rather than forcing a vote.

Use observation-level codes first. For the participant-level tally, mixed directional evidence becomes `uncertain` with both source sets; each person counts at most once per hypothesis. Follow-ups add evidence, not new participants. Keep pending coding separate from zero. A second human reviewer can inspect ambiguous records when available; document who coded and any disagreements, without inventing inter-rater reliability. Synthesis verdicts are `supported-in-sample`, `contradicted`, `mixed` or `unresolved`, not market validation.

## 5. Message and manual-record studies

Only after neutral questions, VP-004a may show the three candidate statements: “You built it. Now meet the people it’s for”; “Turn real customer conversations into a clearer next move”; “Start with one useful conversation. Decide what to try next.” Rotate ABC/BCA/CAB by participant order, with the same presentation size and explanation. Record order, previous product exposure and participant overlap. Ask what result they expect, what they would do first and how this relates to the recent episode already described. Those hypothetical expectations are comprehension data only. Record any real next step offered/accepted/completed/declined separately; no compulsory signup, forced preference or causal conversion claim.

VP-009’s manual record contains: participant relevance; source/date; observation; interpretation; counterevidence/unknowns; requested/agreed/completed action; decision to change/retain/test/stop; next action and review date. The founder fills it from their own permitted material before the facilitator suggests content. Record any facilitator contribution verbatim or in labeled paraphrase. A reviewer sees only the exact cleared snapshot, never the live private notes. If review is refused/unavailable, record that limit; do not substitute a fictional reviewer response. This is a concept test, not a claim that source-linked review shipped in the app.

## 6. Pilot: day zero, seven and fourteen

Enroll three founders under the same eligibility and separate follow-up choice. Record onboarding time as day zero, signup date separately, starting decision, last recruitment attempt, current tools, planned course obligations and actual device/path. Aim for two to three relevant conversations; do not force a quota, outreach to inappropriate people or a positive commitment. A friend is relevant only when their experience fits the problem. Below five completed app interviews, use a labeled manual decision record instead of bypassing the synthesis gate.

| Measure               | Pre-specified definition                                                                                                                           | Report                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Activation by day 7   | At least one completed conversation with a person whose experience relates to the stated problem, within seven days of pilot onboarding            | Confirmed n / all enrolled N; relevance/source, missing/withdrawn and route separately. Not signup or generated questions.             |
| Useful loop by day 14 | Completed external action plus a specific founder-owned changed/retained decision tied to observations, counterevidence/unknowns and a next action | Source-linked n/N; include decisions to narrow, stop or investigate. A summary alone is insufficient.                                  |
| Repeat value          | A voluntary return or second loop                                                                                                                  | Distinguish self-initiated return before any reminder from course-required, researcher-reminded and unknown. Report dates and trigger. |
| Follow-through        | Requested/agreed/completed trial, introduction, use, return or payment                                                                             | Separate each action and its state; do not collapse into a validation score.                                                           |
| Reviewer value        | A reviewer uses a cleared record for a concrete feedback decision                                                                                  | Actual action, source and assistance; absent review remains absent.                                                                    |

The human may conduct the agreed day-seven and day-fourteen check-ins. No additional reminder is assumed; record every actual reminder, support interaction and course deadline. At each contact ask what happened since the previous episode before offering coaching. Use path labels `app`, `manual_self_completed`, `manual_facilitated`, `mixed`; assistance labels `none`, `logistics`, `navigation`, `method_coaching`, `facilitator_suggested_decision`, with minutes and affected step. A founder must be able to explain the decision; copying a facilitator’s conclusion is not independent decision value.

**Continuation rule:** if at least **2 of the 3 enrolled founders** complete a useful loop by day fourteen, consider another bounded test of the observed workflow. This is a pragmatic planning threshold, not validation, statistical significance or proof of self-service app value. Report mode/help beside it: two assisted/manual successes justify investigating that form of support, not claiming app efficacy. Unprompted repeat value remains a separate result and informs the next decision. Do not alter the rule after seeing results.

Use all enrolled participants in the headline denominator, including withdrawals/missing follow-up as separately reported states, without treating missing outcomes as failure or success. Also show observed n/N and data removed after withdrawal. If fewer than three enroll or data are too incomplete, report inconclusive; do not quietly replace non-completers. Any later cohort is separately dated and counted. An informative negative result should change the next test, not be hidden.

## 7. Phone preflight and fallback

Before the session, on the intended phone/browser using fictional non-sensitive data, check the actual deployed revision: email sign-in/return, correct project, contact entry, readable questions/keyboard, back/next, final save, reload and correct saved content. Inspect network-loss/navigation risk without risking participant notes. Check generation only if a bounded authorized provider call is available; a paid call is not required for protocol preparation. Record `pass | fail | not_run`, device/browser, revision/date, step and evidence; a desktop build is not a phone pass.

At this base, questions start with AI, notes save only at wrap-up, consent is not persisted and some regeneration replaces existing work. Do not put participant material into that flow merely to dogfood. Use the app for capture only when the exact permissions, privacy, reliability and question-set prerequisites are satisfied. Otherwise choose paper or a private document with AI disabled, paste/type the fixed guide manually, and use a separate consent record. A failed preflight leads to `manual_self_completed` or `manual_facilitated`, not an unplanned code change or invented pass. Record the failure as usability/operational evidence, not founder demand.

Do not regenerate outreach or overwrite wanted data for the pilot. The full proposed Marketing Lab, public fictional demo and classroom exercise are different exposures; record what was actually shown and do not count fictional channels/results as founder outcomes.

## 8. Amendments, reporting and completion

Before each new cohort record protocol version/date, reason, prospective change and affected measures. Preserve original question responses/codes; append a correction with the old/new interpretation and source, rather than silently recoding to fit the pitch. Privacy withdrawal can remove content; retain only a non-identifying methods note where permitted.

Report approached/screened/eligible/consented/started/completed/declined/withdrawn/missing counts, without contact identities; distinguish participants from sessions. Show course/self-directed/mixed context, cohort and assistance, and disclose recruiting failures. Each hypothesis has an explicit coded denominator and source ids; tiny convenience-sample fractions are descriptive, not population estimates. Human evidence work remains not started until actual permitted notes exist. Completing VP-008 prepares the studies; it does not pass the research gate.
