# Human-owned recruitment and session materials

**VP-008 · protocol 1.0 · 2026-10-18.** Drafts only. No person was contacted and no participant was recruited by this task. A human fills the concrete scheduling/privacy details and sends only through an appropriate channel. See the [protocol](value-test-plan.md), [founder guide](interviews/guide-founders.md) and [instructor guide](interviews/guide-instructors.md).

## Founder invitation draft

> I’m learning how student founders find people to try a working app and decide what to do next. I’m looking for students aged 18 or over who have a live app or usable prototype, recently tried to recruit users, and have few or no returning users. Could we discuss your most recent experience for about 20–30 minutes? I’m interested in approaches that worked as well as those that did not. This is voluntary research, separate from grades or buying a product. We can use a private conversation/manual note format; AI and sharing are separate choices. If you are interested, reply to [human contact] for the details and screening questions.

Do not promise compensation, free services, endorsement or access that has not been arranged. Do not call a participant a customer or buyer because they reply. Avoid giving a preferred “fear/confidence” diagnosis in the invitation.

## Founder screening, privately recorded

1. Are you an adult student (18+) and responsible for this app/prototype?
2. Can a potential user currently try its core interaction? A private demonstration is sufficient; do not require a public URL or credentials.
3. What specific recruitment effort did you make in the last 30 days, and when? A concrete blocked attempt may qualify; a future plan alone does not.
4. To the best of your knowledge, how many distinct people used it on at least two occasions in the previous 14 days? What is the basis and what is unknown? The first sample uses 0–10 as an operational “few” range; unresolved counts are not zero.
5. Is this work course-related, self-directed or mixed? What deadlines/check-ins apply? What is your relationship to the researcher?

Mark eligible/adjacent/unresolved and why. Seek both course and self-directed contexts and counterexamples; do not screen for agreement with the product promise. Do not collect participant contacts in a repository or upload screening answers to AI without that separate permission.

## Instructor/program-lead invitation draft

> I’m learning how entrepreneurship educators set and review customer-discovery work. I’m looking for a recent assignment or review episode, the tools/process you used, and what happened after your feedback. Would you consider a voluntary 20–30-minute conversation? I’m interested in what already works as well as gaps. No endorsement, purchase or identifiable student work is requested. Notes, AI processing and any selected sharing would be separate choices. If interested, reply to [human contact] for details.

Screen for a concrete episode in the last 12 months, role in reviewing/adopting resources, broad context and any past approval-process experience. Budget authority may be unknown or held by someone else. Keep workshop recruitment labeled as a convenience source, not institutional participation.

## Permission and session card

Before taking content notes, the human fills and explains:

- Who runs the study, named note access, private storage, review/deletion dates and the participant’s contact/withdrawal route.
- Manual note-taking: explicit granted/declined answer. No audio/video recording. No retained content if notes are declined.
- Optional external AI: selected material, exact service/provider route, purpose, handling limits and a separate explicit answer; otherwise keep analysis manual.
- Optional quote/artifact sharing: exact preview and intended audience/use, separately granted or declined. No default public quote or instructor access.
- Optional follow-up: specific purpose/timing. Consent to an interview is not consent to later contacting or product outreach.

Record each choice with date, protocol version and scope in the private log. Unknown/historical missing choices are not granted. Explain that stopping participation does not affect a grade, and carry that through the actual recruiting/teaching arrangements. Do not suggest that external processing or already shared copies can always be recalled.

## Phone preflight and manual kit

Before the interview, using fictional data, record date/device/browser/deployed revision and `pass | fail | not_run` for sign-in, correct project, questions, mobile readability/keyboard, back/next, final save and reload. Check the actual permissions and AI context before any generation; no paid test is required just to prepare this kit. Never risk live participant notes to test refresh/offline behavior.

Prepare a private paper/document copy of the fixed guide, the permission card, blank source-id note template and manual decision record. Disable transcription/AI in the tool. If app capture fails or lacks the needed permissions/question setup, use this kit and label the actual path/help. Do not silently repair code or count the manual experience as app usability. Below five completed interviews, the manual decision record is the intended option; do not invent interviews to unlock synthesis.

## Follow-up drafts, only after specific opt-in

**Day seven:** “For the follow-up you agreed to, what happened since we last spoke? Which action did you take, what did you learn, and what remains uncertain? If you have not returned to it, that is useful to know too. You can skip or stop.” Ask before coaching and record any reminder effect.

**Day fourteen:** “What happened after your next step? What decision did you make or keep, what observations informed it, and what will you do next? What did you do independently and where did you use help?” Record missing outcomes honestly; no required positive result.

**Artifact/review:** “You mentioned an artifact you might choose to show. If you still want to, we can review the specific redacted version and agree who may see it first. There is no obligation.” An agreement is pending until received; receipt is research cooperation, not purchasing intent.

A participant may forward a neutral invitation if they choose. Do not ask for another person’s contact details, send automated reminders or enroll referrals without their own choice. The agent creates these materials; human research tasks remain unperformed.
