# Assessment · reasoning and participant respect

## Four criteria, 0-3 each · 12 points total

Use for the case, fieldwork or private alternative. A well-reasoned stop or honestly unrun test can earn full credit. Explain expectations in advance. No automated grading or authenticity score is supplied.

| Criterion                                | 3 · clear                                                                                                | 2 · developing                                             | 1 · needs revision                                   | 0 · missing                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Question quality                         | Specific recent behavior/workaround; neutral, useful follow-up                                           | Mostly concrete; one leading or vague part                 | Mainly pitches, hypotheticals or general opinions    | No question to review                                            |
| Source vs interpretation                 | Traceable source/type/date; interpretation distinct; counterevidence and unknowns retained               | Distinction mostly clear; one source or limit missing      | Claims outrun source; disagreement lost              | No usable distinction or source                                  |
| Revision and decision                    | Explains changed/retained/stopped decision, feedback and next test; acknowledges alternative explanation | Reasoning present but next step or feedback link weak      | Cosmetic change or unsupported conclusion            | No decision/reflection                                           |
| Participant respect and honest reporting | Appropriate permissions/data minimization; truthful asset; simulation, unknowns and help labeled         | Practices sound with a stated gap to resolve before acting | Missing access/data check or unclear evidence status | No respect/reporting plan; fabricated material presented as real |

**Do not grade:** customer/revenue totals, network, spending, AI use, polish, positive validation or agreement with the instructor. Respect alternative formats and nondisclosure. A failed test can show sound reasoning; a successful count can have weak evidence.

**Feedback:** one supported source/decision, one uncertainty, one next action the student can choose. Ask for an explanation before assuming misconduct. Correct fabricated data through established institutional processes; this worksheet cannot verify that an interview occurred.

## Pedagogical rationale and limits

The owner’s BUS131 “Fail Faster, Fail Smarter,” Week 6 slides inform this sequence: own structure before AI (pp. 4-5), audience/places (6-8), two routes and measures (12-14), tasks/resources (16-17), peer walkthrough/revision/reflection (20-24). This is curriculum provenance, not an effectiveness study.

Patricia Greene’s [Four Approaches to Teaching an Entrepreneurship Method](https://entrepreneurship.babson.edu/four-approaches-to-teaching-an-entrepreneurship-method/) (Babson, February 14, 2024) discusses practice, design and reflection. It informs the lesson’s rationale; it is not endorsement or evidence of First Ten’s efficacy.

The EEF’s [Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition) (second edition, November 13, 2025) is school-level guidance on planning, monitoring and evaluation. Applying it here is a design inference; do not transfer school attainment effects to university entrepreneurs or this app. Sources checked October 18, 2026.
