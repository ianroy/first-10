First Ten helps a student founder turn customer conversations into a clearer next move. It makes the reasoning visible: observation, interpretation, uncertainty and a proposed action.

In fictional StudyLoop, Maya's concern is knowing who will attend, while Nora's existing group already works. The next experiment might be attendance confirmation for existing groups, rather than another way to discover strangers. No customer result is implied.

The Next 50 workflow develops that learning into a $0 marketing test: an audience, one main channel, useful content, a target and reflection. Students think first; optional AI helps develop drafts they must check. The process draws on experiential learning, scaffolding, formative peer feedback, metacognition and responsible AI. A proposed 60-minute lesson leads to a one-week test and reflection.

Explore the example and adapt the lesson. The aim is a better next question and a defensible next action. We have not yet established learning gains or product demand.
