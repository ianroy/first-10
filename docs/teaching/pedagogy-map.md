# Teaching built into the workflow

**MKT-001 · 2026-10-18 · design map, not an effectiveness finding.** Based on repository `07f704ddfb84a8d5c65633703079e97f783d5981`, the owner’s BUS131 Week 6 slides and the sources below. This map supplies the landing page, lesson and pitch deck with named concepts, concrete activities and honest implementation status. The [Marketing Lab contract](../marketing/marketing-lab-contract.md) owns fields and behavior; [marketing claims](../marketing/claims.md) owns the public-copy release checks.

The intended lesson is that a student can connect a customer observation to an audience choice, a reachable channel, a truthful asset, a small test and a revised decision. AI can assist after the student’s first thinking. Neither the example nor this mapping demonstrates improved learning, customer acquisition or Babson endorsement.

## 3. A concrete teaching sequence

Use the student’s real app and relevant, permitted observations for an actual activity. Use the fully labeled fictional StudyLoop case for rehearsal; it cannot substitute for customer evidence. Do not require public disclosure, a large network, paid printing/travel or AI access to complete the exercise.

1. **Think and organize:** give the problem and a $0/new-cash constraint. Students write their own decisions/tasks and group them before seeing a supplied structure or using AI. Record audience, useful action and first channel ideas.
2. **Investigate:** select a specific beachhead and distinguish an observation from an assumption. Find an exact place/link and inspect fit, access and rules. Unknown is a legitimate field state.
3. **Choose and resource:** pick one primary and one alternative route. Budget time, existing equipment/access, support work and any existing AI allowance; state a no-cost fallback.
4. **Make and review:** create one channel-appropriate asset. The student may now ask AI for a bounded draft. Check the promise, builder disclosure, destination and useful action. A peer walks the route; capture behavior and make one revision.
5. **Plan and run:** assign a small task with an owner, date, prerequisite and definition of done. Define the one-week outcome, baseline/unknown, measurement source and repeat/revise/stop criteria before manual outreach.
6. **Observe and reflect:** keep reach, visits, use, return and referral distinct; name dates, sources and denominators. Explain a changed or retained decision, limitations and the next action. An incomplete test is reported as incomplete.

This sequence follows the owner’s activity intent without forcing every learner to use identical headings. A lesson can teach it manually before the in-app lab ships; label that difference. The public demo illustrates source → interpretation → counterevidence → choice → test, then channel → content → measurement. No generated text, fictional count or choice click becomes research or adoption evidence.

## 5. What to inspect before claiming delivery

The implementation check is observable: original thinking preserved; scaffolds/AI offered afterward; two channels with unresolved access visible; reviewed asset connected to a working destination; peer versus self check distinguished; unknown measurements preserved; denominators named; reflection can choose stop. Verify these in the actual UI and manual lesson independently.

A later educational study would need a defined question, appropriate participants/comparison, ethical data handling, measures and analysis beyond these design checks. No such study occurred in MKT-001. Do not confuse completed tasks, polished plans, teacher interest, workshop clicks or classroom compliance with learning gains or voluntary product demand.
