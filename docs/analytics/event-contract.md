# Product events and funnel contract

**AN-001 · version 1 · 2026-10-18 · source audit: `c00327c`.** This is the implementation contract for AN-002 and ADM-001, not deployed instrumentation, an authorization to activate telemetry, or evidence of adoption. Definitions apply prospectively from a recorded instrumentation start time. Historical row counts must not be relabeled as events.

First Ten adoption and a founder's customer acquisition are different populations. Report them in separate panels with their own sources, units and observation windows. A saved interview, accepted draft or return is observed app behavior; none establishes interview authenticity, learning improvement, product–market fit or willingness to pay.

## 2. Identity, clock and collection rules

- **Account subject:** one analytics pseudonym for a freshly verified account ID. Store the ID mapping in a restricted server table, never in public events or client-visible dashboards. Multiple projects do not create multiple account subjects. Project metrics use a separate opaque project reference, with ownership verified at the event's transaction.
- **Guest principal subject:** one pseudonym scoped to the verified `class_id` + `principal_id`. Class and team references are independently scoped opaque IDs. Do not copy display aliases. Principal recovery retains this subject; a new principal admitted with a team-return/invite capability is a new principal, even if the same human returns. Report “guest principals,” never unique students. A team metric counts team IDs, separately from principals.
- **No identity merging:** do not link account and guest subjects, different classes, browser sessions or household devices by name, email, IP, similar content or timing. Future deliberate account transfer needs its own access/consent contract. Team membership does not authorize attributing all team edits to every member.
- **Optional public attribution:** off until a distinct, understandable analytics choice is implemented. After opt-in, use a random in-memory tab-session token, rotating after 30 minutes of inactivity and at 24 hours at the latest. It disappears on tab close/reload. No fingerprinting, IP-derived identifiers, cross-site cookies, advertising IDs or silent persistent identifiers. This intentionally undercounts repeat browsing. Declining must preserve the public demo and no-account classroom experience.
- **Separate permission:** interview note-taking, AI processing and sharing choices are neither analytics opt-in nor authority to read content. Teacher provisioning does not silently opt students into product tracking. The implementation must decide and disclose the permitted operational measurement basis separately; this contract makes no legal conclusion.
- **Authoritative times:** `occurred_at` is database/server UTC time at the verified durable transition; `received_at` is server receipt UTC time. Public visit/activity claims carry server receipt time and `source_kind=client_observation`, not a claimed authoritative occurrence time. No client clock determines cohort eligibility. Display timezone and inclusive/exclusive bounds explicitly.
- **Exactly once effect:** transactional changes write a minimal event/outbox record in the same commit. Enforce a database unique dedup key; relay retries retain `event_id`. Rollback emits no success. Client retries, reconnects, React remounts, refreshes and operation receipt replays cannot create another success event. Denied/failed requests belong in separately bounded security/operational logs, not successful funnel stages.
- **No retrospective invention:** label legacy rows as `legacy_state_as_of` in a separate report. Never invent signup, acceptance, completion or first-return times. Migration/import of old work is not a new activation. The first observed action for a preexisting subject is not necessarily their lifetime first action.

## 4. Reporting windows and denominators

Use UTC day cohorts, `[start, end)` boundaries and a visible `as_of`. An account cohort starts at `account_created`; a guest cohort starts at `guest_principal_admitted`, within one class. Include only post-instrumentation cohorts with complete event coverage, an eligible declared measurement basis and trusted `environment=production`, `population=ordinary`. Show synthetic, fixture, operator and unknown populations separately; never guess that an ordinary-looking email is a real participant.

Each report includes numerator `n`, denominator `N`, observation window, mature/immature counts, measurement-eligible population, excluded fixture count, withdrawals, unknown coverage and source version. `N=0` displays “no eligible cohort,” never 0% conversion. Failed collection or missing source means **unavailable**, not zero. Do not multiply independent rates into an invented end-to-end funnel.

## 6. Strict privacy field allowlist

Reject unknown fields and nested free-form metadata. A v1 product event may contain only:

- `event_id`, `event_name`, `schema_version=1`, `occurred_at`, `received_at`, `source_kind` (`database_transition`, `trusted_auth`, `client_observation`), fixed `producer_version`;
- `environment` and trusted `population` (`ordinary`, `synthetic`, `operator`, `unknown`), `subject_kind` (`account`, `guest_principal`, `public_session`), opaque `subject_ref`;
- event-specific, verified opaque `project_ref`, `class_ref`, `team_ref`, `activity_ref`, `artifact_ref`; a bounded integer revision only where needed for dedup;
- allowlisted `route_category`, `activity_category`, `audience`, `entry_method`, `evidence_basis`, `measurement_choice_version`; bounded random `session_nonce` only for the session event;
- optional acquisition enum **only after separate attribution opt-in:** `direct_or_unknown`, `workshop_qr`, `educator_invite`, `course_link`. Unknown campaign/query values are discarded, never stored as text. Do not copy a guest join code or complete referrer to identify a channel.

Store dedup receipts in a restricted producer namespace. IDs/pseudonyms are still potentially linkable data, not “anonymous” by assertion. Omit every inapplicable field; reject client-supplied identities/roles/population classifications. Authentication/session secrets stay exclusively in their existing authorized transport.

**Never collect:** names, aliases, emails, contact IDs/details, relationship/relevance/bias text, interview questions/notes/quotes/tags, hypothesis/learning/action text, evidence/source excerpts, prompt/output contents, URLs/repository paths, query strings/fragments, full referrers, IPs or abuse hashes, user-agent/device fingerprint, exact location, auth/cookie/CSRF/join/recovery secrets, provider keys, raw exception payloads, payment claims or inferred demographics. Event ingestion and validation errors must not log rejected payloads. Operational AI accounting/audit records keep their existing separate purpose and access boundaries; analytics does not authorize joining their contents.

## 7. Proposed storage, access and retention — not implemented

AN-002 must implement and verify these defaults before activation; no deletion guarantee is made by this document:

1. Raw events, delivery dedup receipts and subject mappings expire after **30 days**; daily deletion is checked by oldest-row age and alerting. Source operations retain their existing durable mutation/completion identity: a producer must not regenerate an expired event from an old successful receipt or a replay. Any additional source-level first-occurrence marker needs an explicit retention/deletion design in AN-002; do not keep an undeclared permanent analytics identity to solve deduplication. Key deletion or dropping a mapping alone is not proof that events were erased.
2. Daily cohort aggregates may last **13 months**, with no subject/artifact IDs and no free text. Suppress cells below 10 subjects, complementary cells/differences and arbitrary filters that could reconstruct them. A privileged audit role is not a license for raw student analytics export. Small classes remain unavailable in the platform funnel; permitted teacher progress is a separate class contract.
3. Measurement withdrawal immediately blocks new optional observations and invalidates queued ones. Delete attributable raw events/mappings under a tested workflow; previously published properly de-identified aggregates cannot promise individual recall. Report aggregate freeze/cutoff dates and denominator revisions. Participant note/AI/sharing withdrawal separately invalidates derived eligibility where applicable; it does not grant analytics consent or retroactively label a completed interview authentic.
4. Project archive, class closure, guest expiry and capability revocation recheck authority before any new collector event. No event collector may restore access or use a service key to scan private documents. Class retention settings and guest access deadlines are not implemented physical deletion schedules. Align the final retention rules with PRV/FERPA review rather than treating this proposal as institutional approval.
5. Raw ingestion/outbox access is server-only with least privilege. Admin delivery checks the current database-backed platform grant and records the existing aggregate audit before responding; no raw rows/subject list is returned. Keep presenter fixtures separate. A teacher role grants access only to the existing scoped class projections and deliberate submissions, never this raw ledger or private-source analytics.

## 8. Delivery and acceptance gates

AN-002 owns the minimal schema/outbox, producer integration and deletion worker; ADM-001 owns the cohort service and truthful dashboard. This contract does not authorize a new analytics vendor, external data transmission, paid service, general AI activation or public tracking.

Before enabling any stage, verify the actual production revision with rollback available:

- Transaction rollback, idempotent retry, concurrent duplicate completion, acceptance replacement and restore cannot double count; denied/archived/expired writes create no success event. Cover direct legacy writes as well as RPC paths.
- Guest principal recovery does not admit again; team-return may create a different principal; unrelated guest/account/class scopes cannot be joined or supplied by the caller. Submission copies do not create new interview completions.
- Optional collection remains off after decline/withdrawal; public demo and class participation still work. No secrets/text appear in events, diagnostics, error reports, URLs or aggregate responses. Test the allowlist with deliberately sensitive rejected fixtures.
- Cohort queries pass boundary tests at exactly 24 hours, 7/8/14 days, UTC midnight, missing sources, zero denominators, partial windows, deletion/withdrawal and fixture exclusion. Prove all stages share the stated population; never backfill an unverifiable timestamp.
- Raw-event TTL, dedup expiry/replay defense, deletion verification and small-cell suppression have real tests; dashboards show instrumentation start, freshness, event contract version and unavailable states. No feature flag alone claims these checks passed.

The pilot's relevance, assistance, voluntary return and evidence-informed decision assessment still require its separately approved human research protocol. Event counts can identify where to investigate; they cannot substitute for that assessment.
