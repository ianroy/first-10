# Authorized AI context, version 2

AI requests for owner-private projects still enter through `generateAI`. Its server adapter calls `get_ai_processing_context_v2` as the verified owner. Class submissions, teaching resources and platform aggregates are explicit future scope types that currently deny access. Instructor or platform-admin status never authorizes student interview notes. The fixed fictional marketing practice route remains separate.

## Permission and record boundary

Project AI processing must be explicitly owner-reported as granted. Interview notes need both note and AI choices; a linked contact also needs its own AI choice. Contact names are excluded. Accepted learnings need their own AI permission and unresolved/withdrawn evidence is conservatively withheld: an ineligible completed interview or contact prevents an accepted learning from entering context. Unknown historical choices remain unknown. These reports do not verify a respondent's identity, authenticity, or legal consent.

The versioned RPC supplies ordered whole records with UUID, creation time, permission revision, content hash and an opaque source ID. Linked contact permission revisions travel as dependencies even when contact text is not selected. The content hash binds the literal allowed field projection and linked permission dependency. Changing source content creates a different source ID. Permission revisions remain separately fenced. No contact name, arbitrary extra interview field, raw provider envelope, or unrelated project is included.

The original records are never shortened or overwritten. A malformed record or one exceeding its RPC cap is omitted as a whole with a categorized count. RPC body caps are 8,000 UTF-8 bytes for the project, 12,000 per interview, 6,000 per learning and 2,000 per contact. Interview answers must be an array of at most 30 exact `{q,note,quote}` objects; tags use the existing five-value allowlist. Candidate limits are 32 interviews, 32 contacts and 16 learnings, newest first with UUID tie-breaking. Non-question tasks fail closed when the total number of potential contacts/interviews/learnings exceeds 500, bounding dependency locks; project-only questions do not read or lock child records. Continue manually at that cap; all originals remain saved.

The pure server assembler sorts deterministically and packs whole records. It allows at most 49 selected records including the mandatory project. Provider context caps are 28,000 bytes total; source sections allow 16,000 interview, 4,000 learning and 2,000 contact bytes. The mandatory project including provider metadata must fit 8,000 bytes. JSON encoding and opaque ID overhead count toward the caps. Records that do not fit are counted as `budget` omissions; no substring or blind JSON slice is sent. The model receives omission counts for permission, oversize, invalid shape, candidate limit and packing limit. It must not infer omitted content or claim complete coverage.

UTF-8 bytes bound payload size. They are **not** a token-count proof. The reviewed route still reserves its full endpoint input token ceiling plus bounded output under AI-004. Existing route approvals and evaluation hashes must be regenerated for this new prompt/schema/context version before any general paid route is enabled.

## Dispatch fence

The trusted server produces a content-free manifest of selected source identities and the exact canonical prompt/schema binding hash. Browser requests cannot supply it. `claim_ai_usage_with_context` uses one transaction and the lock order global budget policy, operation, owner key-share, project, stable contact/interview/learning rows, then scoped permission rows. The weak owner lock protects deletion without introducing an exclusive lock inverse to legacy user-FK inserts. Project and child locks serialize legacy direct row writes as well as current consent RPC writes. All potential existing dependencies are locked within the 500-row cap, including unselected records needed for conservative learning eligibility. New child inserts wait on the locked project's FK. The claim rechecks current ownership, archive state, mandatory project content/permission, each selected source's literal-content hash/permission and linked contact permission revisions, then runs the existing one-use budget claim. Stale or withdrawn context cannot receive a dispatch acknowledgment. Duplicate and lost acknowledgments never authorize another call.

The fence defines dispatch authorization. A withdrawal or edit ordered after a successful claim cannot recall information already authorized/in flight or transmitted to a provider. The fresh pre-claim RPC reload improves recovery messaging, while the locked database check is authoritative. A failed fence retains/cancels holds only under the existing conservative accounting rules; no automatic paid retry occurs.

## Structured grounding and compatibility

Frozen private-v1 contracts remain unchanged. New private-v2 contracts ask for `{draft,grounding}`. Draft fields keep the legacy UI shape. Every string leaf needs an observation, inference or proposal label. Synthesis quotation paths must refer to an authorized interview source and match an entire explicitly recorded `answers[].quote`, with whitespace normalization only. Notes and accepted learning text cannot be passed off as respondent quotations. Unknown IDs, invented/paraphrased quotes, missing or duplicate paths and source-free inferences are rejected. An inference needs at least one selected interview or learning; project/contact hypotheses support proposals only. Questions are proposals. An honest empty quotation array is valid when there is no authorized quote.

Missing evidence must disclose unverified authenticity and any omitted sources. These mechanical checks establish literal traceability, not factual truth, sound inference, or respondent authenticity. Human judgment and classroom discussion remain necessary. Legacy cards receive the unwrapped draft; a full source-inspection UI is future work. The validated classifications and missing-evidence messages are currently internal, not a claim that every existing card displays those annotations.

Known billed usage is settled even when grounding validation fails. No successful output is returned without both a valid draft and its metadata receipt. Private dispatch receipts store only source UUIDs, opaque IDs, hashes, permission revisions and omission counts. Grounding receipts store only typed output paths and source IDs; no quotations, prompts, generated drafts or explanatory messages. Version-2 metadata must match its settled generation's model/provider/cost/token counts. Existing v1 metadata remains readable. Receipts do not retroactively certify copied/manual summaries or bind edits made after draft generation. Content lineage and privacy deletion/purge remain separate work under PRV-002; append-only accounting identifiers are retained, not silently deleted.
